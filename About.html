About SquadSync

Where every gamer finds their perfect squad.

🎯 Our Mission

SquadSync was built to solve a real problem: finding friendly, non-toxic gamers to play with. No more joining random lobbies with strangers. No more awkward Discord servers. Just real matches based on your vibe.

⚙️ How It Works

Create your Vibe Profile, select your favourite game, tell us whether you prefer voice chat or text, and pick the days you're available. Our matchmaking system pairs you with students who share the same playstyle and schedule.

🛡️ Safety First

Every user verifies their student email address. Our reporting system keeps the community safe. We don't track followers or popularity — just good matches with good people.

💜 Our Values

Inclusion, empathy, and respect. Whether you're a competitive ranked player or a casual explorer, there's a squad waiting for you.
